Welcome to our very first episode! In this episode, we introduce Hugo, the fast and flexible static site generator built with Go.

## What is Hugo?

Hugo is a static site generator that takes your content and templates and generates a complete website. It's known for its speed and flexibility, making it perfect for blogs, documentation sites, and portfolios.

## Topics Covered

- **Why Hugo?** - Speed, simplicity, and flexibility
- **Installation** - Getting Hugo up and running
- **Basic concepts** - Content, templates, and configuration
- **First site** - Building your first Hugo site

## Key Features Discussed

- Lightning-fast build times
- Flexible content organization
- Powerful templating system
- Great documentation and community

Join us as we explore the world of static site generation with Hugo!
